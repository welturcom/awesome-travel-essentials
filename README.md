# Awesome Travel Essentials [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of official portals, apps and trusted resources for traveling anywhere in the world: visas and entry permits, safety, health, transport, money and responsible travel.

Entry rules are changing fast. Europe now registers travelers at its borders, the UK requires an ETA, and much of Asia has moved to digital arrival cards. Look-alike agent sites charge extra for forms that are often free. This list points you to the **official** source first.

Maintained by **[Weltur](https://www.weltur.com)**, tours and activities worldwide. Contributions welcome, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Rules and fees change often. Always confirm on the official site before you travel. Last full review: **October 2026**.

## Visas & Entry Requirements

### Check What You Need

- [IATA Travel Centre](https://www.iatatravelcentre.com/) - Passport, visa and health entry requirements by nationality and destination, from the database airlines use.
- [Check if you need a UK visa or ETA](https://www.gov.uk/check-uk-visa) - Official UK checker.

### Electronic Travel Authorizations

Visa-free travelers increasingly need a pre-approved authorization before boarding. Apply only on the official site; agent sites charge more.

- [ETIAS (Schengen Area)](https://travel-europe.europa.eu/etias_en) - Upcoming EU travel authorization for visa-exempt visitors. **Not yet launched**: as of late 2026 there is no confirmed start date, and the EU will announce it months in advance. Ignore sites that already sell "ETIAS".
- [EES — Entry/Exit System (Schengen Area)](https://travel-europe.europa.eu/ees_en) - Digital border registration (fingerprints and photo) for non-EU visitors. Fully operational since 10 April 2026; it replaces passport stamping.
- [UK ETA](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/apply-for-an-electronic-travel-authorisation-eta) - Required for visa-free visitors to the UK, including children and travelers who pass through UK border control in transit.
- [US ESTA](https://esta.cbp.dhs.gov/) - Required for Visa Waiver Program travelers to the United States.
- [Canada eTA](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/eta.html) - Required for visa-exempt visitors flying to or through Canada.
- [Australia ETA (subclass 601)](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/electronic-travel-authority-601) - Apply through the official AustralianETA app.
- [New Zealand NZeTA](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/visas/new-zealand-electronic-travel-authority-nzeta/) - Required for visa-waiver travelers and most transit passengers. Includes the visitor conservation and tourism levy (IVL).
- [South Korea K-ETA](https://www.k-eta.go.kr/) - Required for visa-free visitors, but many nationalities are temporarily exempt until 31 December 2026.
- [Sri Lanka ETA](https://www.eta.gov.lk/) - Official electronic travel authorization for Sri Lanka.
- [Kenya ETA](https://www.etakenya.go.ke/) - Official electronic travel authorization for Kenya.

### Digital Arrival Cards

Free online declarations submitted shortly before arrival. They are not visas.

- [All Indonesia Arrival Card](https://allindonesia.imigrasi.go.id/) - Indonesia. Combines immigration, customs and health declarations; submit within 3 days before arrival.
- [Malaysia Digital Arrival Card (MDAC)](https://imigresen-online.imi.gov.my/mdac/main) - Malaysia. Submit no more than 3 days before arrival.
- [SG Arrival Card](https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-transit-depart/entering-singapore/sg-arrival-card) - Singapore. Submit within 3 days before arrival on the ICA website or the MyICA app.
- [Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC)](https://tdac.immigration.go.th/) - Thailand. Free; beware of look-alike `tdac` domains that charge fees.
- [Visit Japan Web](https://services.digital.go.jp/en/visit-japan-web/) - Japan. Optional QR codes for immigration and customs. Japan's own travel authorization (JESTA) is not expected before 2028.

### e-Visas

- [India e-Visa](https://indianvisaonline.gov.in/evisa/) - Official Government of India e-Visa portal.
- [Vietnam e-Visa](https://evisa.immigration.gov.vn/) - Official Vietnam Immigration Department portal.
- [Indonesia e-Visa / e-VOA](https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id/) - Official Indonesian immigration portal.
- [Türkiye e-Visa](https://www.evisa.gov.tr/) - Official Republic of Türkiye e-Visa site.
- [Egypt e-Visa](https://visa2egypt.gov.eg/) - Official Egyptian e-Visa portal.
- [Saudi Arabia e-Visa](https://visa.visitsaudi.com/) - Official tourist e-Visa from Visit Saudi.
