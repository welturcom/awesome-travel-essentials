# Indonesia

> A curated list of official portals, apps, permits and trusted resources for traveling in Indonesia — from Bali and Lombok to Komodo, Java and beyond.

## Before You Go: Entry & Visas

- [Indonesia e-Visa (official)](https://evisa.imigrasi.go.id/) - Directorate General of Immigration portal for e-VOA (electronic visa on arrival) and other visas. The only official visa site — the domain ends in `imigrasi.go.id`.
- [All Indonesia Arrival Card](https://allindonesia.imigrasi.go.id/) - Mandatory, free digital arrival declaration for international arrivals. Combines immigration, customs and health declarations into one QR code. Submit within 3 days before arrival. Not a visa.
- [e-CD Customs Declaration](https://ecd.beacukai.go.id/) - Standalone customs form, still used at entry points not yet covered by All Indonesia.
- [Love Bali — Tourist Levy (official)](https://lovebali.baliprov.go.id/) - Pay Bali's one-time foreign tourist levy (IDR 150,000 per person, including children) before you fly and keep the QR voucher. Also used to apply for exemptions at least 5 days before arrival.
- [Directorate General of Immigration](https://www.imigrasi.go.id/) - Official news, visa types and immigration office locations.
- [Indonesian Embassies Abroad](https://kemlu.go.id/) - Ministry of Foreign Affairs; find the embassy or consulate for your country.

## Getting Around

### Flights & Airports

- [InJourney Airports](https://www.injourneyairports.id/) - Operator of most major airports, including Bali (DPS), Lombok (LOP), Jakarta (CGK) and Labuan Bajo (LBJ).
- [Garuda Indonesia](https://www.garuda-indonesia.com/) - National flag carrier, domestic and international routes.

### Trains

- [KAI — Kereta Api Indonesia](https://www.kai.id/) - National railway for Java and Sumatra; book through the Access by KAI app or website.
- [Whoosh High-Speed Rail](https://kcic.co.id/) - Jakarta–Bandung high-speed train.

### Ferries & Boats

- [Ferizy (ASDP)](https://ferizy.com/) - Official online tickets for state ferries, including Padangbai (Bali) – Lembar (Lombok), Ketapang – Gilimanuk (Java – Bali) and Sape – Labuan Bajo. Most covered ports no longer sell tickets at the counter.
- [PELNI](https://www.pelni.co.id/) - National passenger shipping line connecting distant islands across the archipelago.

### Ride-Hailing & Local Transport

- [Gojek](https://www.gojek.com/) - Rides, food and deliveries.
- [Grab](https://www.grab.com/id/en/) - Rides, food and deliveries.
- [Traveloka](https://www.traveloka.com/) - Domestic flights, trains, hotels and attractions.
- [tiket.com](https://www.tiket.com/) - Domestic flights, trains, hotels and attractions.

## National Parks & Permits

- [SiORA — Komodo National Park](https://siora.id/) - Official reservation system run by the Komodo National Park office. Since April 2026 all visits must be pre-booked here (or through a licensed operator) under a daily visitor quota; there are no walk-in tickets.
- [Mount Rinjani National Park](https://www.rinjaninationalpark.id/) - Official park site with climbing procedures, licensed trekking organizers, closures and the eRinjani booking app. Check park news before planning: access is often restricted during fire season.
- [UNESCO World Heritage Sites in Indonesia](https://whc.unesco.org/en/statesparties/id) - Borobudur, Prambanan, Komodo, Lorentz, the Bali subak landscape and more.

## Safety, Weather & Natural Hazards

- [BMKG](https://www.bmkg.go.id/) - Official weather, earthquake and tsunami warnings.
- [MAGMA Indonesia](https://magma.esdm.go.id/) - Official volcano alert levels (Agung, Rinjani, Bromo, Semeru, Merapi and others). Respect exclusion zones.
- [BNPB](https://bnpb.go.id/) - National Disaster Management Agency.

## Money & Payments

- [Bank Indonesia](https://www.bi.go.id/) - Central bank; official exchange-rate reference and information on the Rupiah (IDR).
- [QRIS](https://www.bi.go.id/QRIS/default.aspx) - Indonesia's national QR payment standard, accepted from street stalls to malls. Some foreign wallets and cross-border QR links now support it.

- [Bali](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Bali) - Temples, rice terraces, surf and the levy you need to pay.

## Responsible Travel

- Dress modestly and wear a sarong and sash when visiting temples; follow posted rules at sacred sites.
- Carry out all your trash on treks — Rinjani blacklists hikers who do not bring their waste down.
- Do not touch or feed wildlife, and keep a safe distance from Komodo dragons; rangers are mandatory for a reason.
- Use reef-safe sunscreen and never stand on coral.
- Book licensed guides and operators, especially for volcano treks and boat trips.
